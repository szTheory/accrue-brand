# Accrue sound

- `accrue-close.wav`, `accrue-confirm.wav`: the raw renders (48 kHz, mono, 16-bit, unmastered), for sound designers and products.
- `listening/accrue-close.mp3`, `listening/accrue-confirm.mp3`: the listening masters (MP3, 320k), tuned for laptop speakers and headphones, at the loudness in `brand.json`. Where sound plays is in the brand book.

## Loudness

The logo sounds are cues, so their master is quieter than the general listening master (−24 LUFS): −36 LUFS, 12 dB lower, because a half-second cue should sit far below speech and music. The raw WAVs sit 4 LU above their masters.
